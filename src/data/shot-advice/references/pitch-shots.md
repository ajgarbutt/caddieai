# Pitch shots

## Standard pitch shot

**Distance:** 30–80 yards.
**When to use:** Most of the distance is carry. Ball on fairway, short grass or clean light rough. Normal pitch scenario with no major complications.
**When it can't be used:** Deep rough, very tight bare lie (hardpan — wedge bounce can skip and thin the ball), low branches requiring a punch shot.

**Club selection:**
- SW: shorter pitches, or when ball needs to stop sooner — avoid from hardpan
- Gap wedge: mid-range pitches
- PW: longer pitches or when more rollout is acceptable; prefer this (or a low flighted pitch) over SW/LW from a tight bare lie

**Set-up:** Ball centre of stance. Feet slightly narrower than full-swing stance. 60% weight on lead foot.
**Technique:** Controlled, shorter swing with a committed follow-through. Let backswing length control distance — do not hit harder for more yardage.
**Swing thought:** Smooth back, smooth through.

## Low flighted pitch

**Distance:** 40–100 yards.
**When to use:** Best when there is room for rollout after landing. Fairway or clean tight lie, especially in wind.
**When it can't be used:** Must carry a bunker, water, steep bank or thick rough directly in front of the green.

**Club selection:**
- PW: most common option
- 9 iron: lower flight and more run
- Gap wedge: when slightly more loft is still needed

**Set-up:** Ball slightly back of centre. Narrow stance, 60% weight on lead foot.
**Technique:** Grip down slightly, shorter swing, controlled finish. Do not swing harder into wind — it increases spin and the ball climbs and balloons.
**Swing thought:** Smooth and low.

---

## High pitch over trouble

**Distance:** 20–60 yards.
**When to use:** Bunker, water, rough or steep rise between ball and landing area. Need the ball to carry over the obstacle and land softly. Ball on fairway, fringe or clean light rough.
**When it can't be used:** Tight bare lie / hardpan (bounce can cause a thin), deep rough, wet turf. Avoid if a simpler standard chip is available — don't play the hero shot when a standard chip does the job.

**Club selection:**
- SW: safer choice for most high pitches on soft/clean turf — not from hardpan
- LW: only when extra height and softest landing are truly required; never from hardpan

**Set-up:** Ball slightly forward of centre. Slightly open stance. Weight favouring lead foot.
**Technique:** Keep face slightly open, maintain the loft, make a committed swing. Do not try to help the ball into the air — trust the loft.
**Swing thought:** Trust the loft and commit.
