# Club yardages

> **For CaddieAI:** These stock distances are reference examples only. Always use the player's saved bag distances from the PLAYER BAG section. Never recommend clubs not in their bag.

Stock carry distances under normal conditions — flat lie, no wind, clean centre strike:

| Club | Carry |
|------|-------|
| 3 hybrid | 200 yards |
| 5 iron | 175 yards |
| 6 iron | 165 yards |
| 7 iron | 155 yards |
| 8 iron | 147 yards |
| 9 iron | 140 yards |
| PW | 130 yards |
| SW | 95 yards |

## Important notes

**No driver** — the 3 hybrid at 200 yards is the longest club in the bag. On tee shots where a driver would normally be used, the 3 hybrid is the go-to. This is actually a useful constraint — it enforces accuracy over distance.

**The gap** — there is a 35-yard gap between PW (130y) and SW (95y). Shots in the 95–130 yard range require partial swings:
- 125–130 yards: full PW
- 115–125 yards: choke down on PW
- 100–110 yards: three-quarter SW
- ~95 yards: full SW

Partial shots carry more uncertainty. When in this range, aim for the middle of the green and accept the distance ambiguity. Don't go at a tight pin from a partial-shot distance.

## Applying adjustments

These are stock distances for a clean strike. Apply the following before recommending a club:

- **Under the shortest stock carry (default):** Pick the shortest usable club in PLAYER BAG (usually SW/LW) and scale the swing — full ≈ stock, three-quarter ≈ 75–80%, half ≈ 50%, chip/putting-stroke when much shorter. Do not grab a longer club (e.g. 135y PW) for a 30y shot and call it a three-quarter swing.
- **Hardpan / tight bare exception:** Wedge bounce (LW/SW/PW) can skip and thin. Prefer 9i or 8i with a gentle stroke.
- **Wind exception (~30–80 yards):** Lower loft + shorter swing for a lower flight that is less caught by the wind; still match swing length to the maths.
- **Mishit buffer**: On full approaches, choose the club that gets there on a 7/10 strike. When in doubt take one more club — but never as an excuse to overswing a short shot with the wrong club.
- **Lie**: Reduce distance for thick rough (10–30%), divots, and tight hardpan. Flyer from light rough may go 5–10% further.
- **Stance**: Uphill adds effective loft (more club / less distance). Downhill reduces loft. Upslope alone does not justify a three-quarter PW from 30 yards.
- **Wind (full shots):** See wind table in shot-factors.md — more club, easier swing into wind.
- **Altitude**: Above ~500m, expect ~5–10% more distance.
