# Ball location

General rules that apply to every shot when the lie is not flat or level relative to the feet.

## Slope

**Upslope** — ball on an upslope effectively adds loft to the club, making the ball go higher and not quite as far.

**Downslope** — ball on a downslope effectively reduces loft on the club, making the ball fly lower and a bit further.

## Ball position relative to feet

**Ball above your feet** — the ball will come out a little left.

**Ball below your feet** — the ball will come out a little right.

When any of these apply, factor them into club choice, target, and what you tell the golfer to expect — even if they only mention slope or foot position briefly.
