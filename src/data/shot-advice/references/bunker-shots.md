# Bunker shots

## Standard greenside bunker shot

**Distance:** 5–25 yards.
**When to use:** Normal lie in a greenside bunker. Priority is escaping safely onto the green.
**When it can't be used:** Plugged lie, very thin lie, or fairway bunker (different technique applies).

**Club selection:**
- SW: standard choice
- LW: only when the bunker lip is high or the pin is very close

**Set-up:** Ball slightly forward of centre. Stance slightly open. Weight favouring lead foot. Dig feet in for stability.
**Technique:** Open clubface slightly. Strike the sand 1–2 inches behind the ball. Keep accelerating through impact — decelerating causes thin or fat strikes.
**Swing thought:** Splash the sand through.

---

## Plugged greenside bunker shot

**Distance:** 5–20 yards.
**When to use:** Ball buried or partly buried in the face or floor of a greenside bunker.
**When it can't be used:** Do not use a normal open-face splash technique — the ball will not come out cleanly.

**Club selection:**
- SW.

**Set-up:** Ball slightly back of centre. Square stance (not open). Weight slightly forward.
**Technique:** Keep face more square than normal. Strike sand close behind the ball with a firm, committed swing. Expect lower launch and more rollout — allow for this when choosing where to land.
**Swing thought:** Commit and get it out.

---

## Long greenside bunker shot

**Distance:** 20–40 yards.
**When to use:** Normal bunker lie but pin is farther away across a larger green.
**When it can't be used:** Poor lie or high lip — in that case the first job is still simply to get out safely. Never sacrifice an escape to chase distance.

**Club selection:**
- SW: standard for a long bunker shot
- Gap wedge: only when the lip is low and clean ball contact is achievable

**Set-up:** Ball slightly forward of centre. Open stance, weight slightly forward.
**Technique:** Same splash concept as a standard bunker shot but with a longer, more committed swing. Take enough sand to lift the ball out — not so much that it falls short.
**Swing thought:** Longer swing, same splash.

---

## Fairway bunker shot

**Distance:** 50–180 yards depending on the lie and lip.
**When to use:** Ball sitting in a fairway bunker with a route forward.
**When it can't be used:** If the lip is too high for the chosen club to clear it safely, take more loft and lay up. The lip is always the first consideration.

**Club selection:**
- Choose enough loft to clear the lip — this comes before distance
- Often take one more lofted club than normal for the yardage (e.g. 7 iron instead of 6 iron)
- If the lip is severe, take a wedge and advance the ball to a safe position

**Set-up:** Ball slightly back of centre. Settle feet into sand for stability. Keep lower-body movement quiet throughout the swing.
**Technique:** Focus on clean ball-first contact. Controlled swing — do not try to hit at full power from sand.
**Swing thought:** Clear the lip first.
